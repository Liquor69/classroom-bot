Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

Classroom Bot is a private personal tool used only by its owner.

Data accessed

With the owner's consent, the app reads (read-only) Google Classroom courses, coursework, course materials, announcements, the owner's own submissions, and Google Drive files attached to them.

Use of data

The data is used only to send notifications and attached files to the owner's private Telegram chat. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Storage

OAuth tokens are stored only in the owner's private environment. No Classroom or Drive data is stored beyond what is needed to send a notification.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Classroom Bot's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
